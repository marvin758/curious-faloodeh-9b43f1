About ISL

A growing distribution partner for everyday life and business.

Island Supply Leaders Distributors is a growing Saint Lucian distribution company serving households, retailers, offices, hotels, resorts, hospitality businesses, service providers and organizations. Our portfolio is not limited to cleaning and car care: we aim to distribute a broad range of quality products, including beverages, household goods, automotive products, hotel and facility cleaning products, housekeeping supplies and other consumer and business supplies.

Our aim is to make dependable products easier to access, expand our range as market needs evolve, and build lasting supplier and customer relationships based on consistency, trust and value.
